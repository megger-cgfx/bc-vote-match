# Privacy

Short version: we built this tool so that it does not need to know who you are, and we would like to keep it that way.

## What we do not collect

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, no login, and no user database. You cannot create an account even if you wanted to.
- **No personal information.** We do not ask for your name, email, address, postal code, age, gender, income, or any other demographic detail. The quiz does not need any of it.
- **Your answers stay in your browser.** Your responses to the 18 statements are computed in your browser and are never transmitted to us or stored on our servers. When you close the tab, they are gone unless you chose to save or share your result yourself.
- **No third-party trackers.** The site loads no advertising networks, no social media pixels, no session replay tools, and no cross-site tracking scripts. There is nothing to opt out of because none of it is there.
- **No data sales or sharing.** We have no data to sell, and we do not share visitor data with parties, candidates, pollsters, or anyone else.

## What little we do keep

The site is static and self-hosted. The web server keeps ordinary server logs: IP address, requested page, timestamp, and browser user agent, rotated on a short schedule and used only for security and aggregate traffic counts (which pages are used, roughly how often).

We use minimal, privacy-respecting, first-party analytics to understand whether the tool works: page views and referrers in aggregate, with no cross-site identifiers and no individual profiles. If we ever change the analytics setup, this page will say so before the change ships.

If you contact us with a correction or a question, we keep your message so we can respond and so we have a record of the correction. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not publish your contact details.

## Sharing your result

If you share your match card on social media or with friends, that is your choice and your disclosure. The share card contains your scores, not your identity, and posting it does not send us anything.

## Children

This tool is aimed at voters, who in BC provincial elections are 18 or older. We knowingly collect no information from anyone, including children.

## Legal posture

**PIPEDA.** As a small Canadian project with no commercial data practices, we treat the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act as our baseline: we collect essentially no personal information, we do not use it for purposes you would not expect, we do not retain it beyond the short server-log rotation, and we do not disclose it to third parties. Questions about personal information can be directed to the contact address in [About](ABOUT.md).

**BC FIPPA.** BC's Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act applies to public bodies. BC Vote Match is not a public body, so FIPIPA does not bind us directly. We nevertheless hold ourselves to the same posture where it matters: data minimization by design, no personal information repositories, no routine disclosure of anything identifiable, and transparency about the small amount of server logging that does occur. If this project were ever operated by or for a public body, a full FIPPA review would precede that change.

## Changes to this page

Any change to our privacy practices will be reflected here first, dated, and described in plain language. Shrinking our data collection does not require an announcement. Growing it does.
