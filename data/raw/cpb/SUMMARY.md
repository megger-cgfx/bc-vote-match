## Leadership timeline (the party had two leaders in 2026)

- 2024 general election: party led by **John Rustad** (its 2024 platform is `cpb-0052`, background only).
- 2026-05-30: **Kerry-Lynne Findlay** wins the leadership on the final ballot, 51.0% to Caroline Elliott's 49.0% (`cpb-0047`).
- 2026-06-16: Findlay appears as Leader on releases (`cpb-0011`).
- 2026-09-20: **Findlay resigns** (`cpb-0032`).
- 2026-09-21: **Lorne Doerkson** (Cariboo-Chilcotin) named **interim** leader (`cpb-0033`).
- 2026-09-22: Premier Eby calls the snap election; Doerkson campaigns on affordability, housing and health care, with DRIPA repeal kept in the platform (`cpb-0012`, `cpb-0035`, `cpb-0036`).
- No permanent leader is in place for the 2026-10-24 election. Doerkson's dated statements are the current party position; Findlay-era 2026 statements stand unless contradicted.

## Gaps and risks for M3

- **No 2026 platform document.** Everything coded as a 2026 party position must cite a specific release, the Policy Declaration, or a Hansard passage — not a platform PDF that does not exist.
- **`cpb-0005` (BC Energy Superpower PDF) has no usable text layer** — it is a design PDF with per-glyph positioning, so `pdftotext` output is unquotable. Read the PDF visually, or quote the HTML release `cpb-0011` and the news coverage (`cpb-0034/0035/0037/0038`) for the same commitments.
- **Leader churn.** Kerry-Lynne Findlay resigned and Lorne Doerkson became **interim** leader in Sept 2026 (`cpb-0027`, `cpb-0028`), days before the snap election. Doerkson's statements are the party's current position; a permanent leader has not been installed.
- **Archive coverage is partial.** Re-run `python3 agents/archive_retry.py --party cpb` later; the local hashed copy is the provenance of record regardless.

## Repo / coordination note

More than one worker wrote `data/raw/cpb/` concurrently (duplicate ids, duplicated urls, stray `cpb-00NN.pdf` orphans). `agents/consolidate_sources.py` dedupes by normalised url and by file content hash, renumbers ids contiguously and deletes orphans; `agents/repair_sources.py` re-fetches any record whose file went missing. **Only one worker should write a given party directory at a time.**
