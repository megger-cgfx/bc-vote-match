What you get

  • Alignment by party

    A percentage per party, computed in your browser from codings that are published in full.

  • A two-dimensional compass

    Economic (left ↔ right) and social (progressive ↔ traditional), with the numbers printed alongside the chart.

  • Statement-by-statement detail

    Your answer and every party’s coded answer, each with the quote and link it came from.

  • Nothing about you leaves your browser

    No accounts, no demographics, no third-party trackers, no analytics that identify you.

BC Vote Match is an educational tool. It is designed to show you where your views sit next to each party's published positions. It does not tell you how to vote and it does not predict your vote.

BC Vote Match is an independent, non-partisan educational project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Vote Compass or Vox Pop Labs, any political party, candidate, or Elections BC. It does not tell you how to vote and it does not predict your vote.
